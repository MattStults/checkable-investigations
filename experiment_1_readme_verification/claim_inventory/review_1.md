## Overall verdict

The inventory is well past a first draft: the tier/snapshot/denominator/scope/supersession skeleton is the right skeleton, the round-trip English-in/English-out discipline on H01–H11 is genuinely useful, Q29's and Q27's own notes anticipate two real traps, and the second addendum's exhaustive-translation rule plus `readme_blame.json` close the selection and provenance holes that would otherwise have been my first complaint. But it is **not fit to freeze as it stands**, and three of the blocking items are not cosmetic: the ∀-hypotheses have no non-vacuity guard (A9), which reproduces inside the inventory the exact defect — "a pass condition that cannot fail is void" — that the document under test claims as its binding lesson; H02/H01 are undecidable at the record's day-level timestamp granularity where 32 of 44 entries share one date (A10); Q16 carries one `tier` for a four-element series the record holds at two tiers, with a `0.40` at both attempt 1 and attempt 3 to collide on (B1); Q04 asserts a negative that the round-4 defensibility column plausibly contradicts with no declared discriminator (B4); H11 quantifies over marked redactions while the record's actual behaviour is unmarked in-place rewriting of retained entries, including the erasure of owner attribution (A14/C4); and the forensic defect-class vocabulary fits four of thirteen errata while three of its nine classes have no instance at all, which makes F02 unanswerable and F01 unanswerable for a different reason — `context_origin` simply is not in the record (C1/C2). Fix those, add H12/H13 for the two missing ∀-claims (A1/A2), add the three missing dimensions (`threshold_rule`, `universe`, `provenance_region`), and pre-declare the named traps — 849/863's do-not-quote rule, the struck 71%, 23/38-vs-23/40, 0.85/0.895 — and the inventory becomes a defensible freeze. Freeze it before that and the experiment will spend its translation budget producing verdicts whose failure modes were knowable in advance.
